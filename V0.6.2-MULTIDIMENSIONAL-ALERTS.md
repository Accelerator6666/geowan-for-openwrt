# GeoWAN v0.6.2 — Multi-dimensional Alert Analytics

## Scope

Extend the v0.6.1 alert-only engine with four additional behavioral correlations, risk metadata, bounded evidence details and read-only LuCI filters. Existing v0.6.1 multi-port access detection remains.

## Rules and conservative defaults

| Rule ID | Default condition | Severity / score |
|---|---|---|
| multi_port_access | One source, at least 3 distinct active public ports and 3 samples in 10 minutes | low / 35 |
| high_frequency_access | One source, at least 20 samples against one active public port in 120 seconds | medium / 60 |
| multi_source_target | At least 8 distinct sources and 12 samples against one active public port in 5 minutes | low / 40 |
| active_blacklist_access | A source still present in the current active GeoWAN ban inventory has at least 2 sampled accesses to active public services in 10 minutes | high / 75 |
| asn_multi_source | At least 4 distinct source IPs and 8 samples in one ASN in 10 minutes | low / 30 |

All thresholds are configurable in /etc/geowan-console/guard-alert-policy.json. Values are bounded in code. A malformed policy disables alert evaluation.

## Evidence and limitations

- All counts are rate-limited kernel-log samples, not complete connection counts.
- The active-blacklist rule correlates only with the current bans inventory. Expired historical ban records are not retained by the current schema and are not claimed to be available.
- ASN is used only to group distinct observed source IPs. ASN, country, city and network organization are not reputation scores.
- The target-concentration and ASN rules store at most 20 source IPs in alert details by default and explicitly mark truncation.
- Alert identity is stable per rule and subject. The 15-minute default cooldown is applied per identity.
- Severity and risk score are triage aids, not a verdict or a prediction of maliciousness.

## Data migration

guard_alerts gains nullable target_port and defaulted details_json, risk_score, and risk_factors columns through additive ALTER TABLE migration. Existing alert rows and v0.5.x tables are preserved.

## UI and RPC

- Read-only Guard overview exposes alert rule/severity filters.
- Alert records include rule, severity, score, target port, evidence details, GeoIP context and occurrence count.
- Alert filters do not change firewall behavior or the existing event/port inventory filters.

## Safety boundary

No nftables command, firewall verdict, DNAT mapping, WAN listener, routing rule, GeoWAN blacklist mutation or automatic block action is added. Alert evaluation is gated on Guard being enabled, in observe mode and having its observation chain installed.
